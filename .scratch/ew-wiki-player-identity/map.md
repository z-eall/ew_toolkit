# Player Identity & Object Ownership — Map

Labels: wayfinder:map

## Destination

Real, signed-off `ew_wiki` content that teaches EWP's player-identity functions (`pid`, `cid`, `platform`, `pname`, `pchar`, `pvisible`) and every source-verified mechanism for determining/recording object ownership in EWP scripting — built from a nearly-complete, source-cited research report, not from scratch.

Reaching the destination means:
1. Page shape decided — how many pages, what each one covers, naming, and which sidebar section (or new section) they live in.
2. Each page built as a `draft: true` prototype in the live dev server, reviewed, and signed off by the maintainer.
3. Only then wired into `astro.config.mjs`'s sidebar and `draft: true` removed, together, in one pass — never split across two passes (see Notes).

- **The research is already done.** Full source-cited report: [research_reports/valheim-ewp-player-identity-ownership-2026-09-14.md](../../research_reports/valheim-ewp-player-identity-ownership-2026-09-14.md) — 6 sections: identity-functions overview, the engine mechanism as a general method (`ClaimOwnership()` vs. passive zone/proximity authority), a verified per-component behavior table, the two genuinely persistent native fields (`Piece.creator`, `Bed.s_owner`), the `<pid>`/`<cid>`-as-key-namespace technique, and other advanced techniques found in the maintainer's own production scripts. Tickets on this map decide *how to turn it into wiki pages*, not re-research it.

## Decisions so far

- [Page shape, naming, and sidebar placement](issues/01-page-shape-and-placement.md) — Two pages. `Advanced Functions - Player Identity` (Advanced tier, last): short primer on `pid`/`cid`/`platform`/`pname`/`pchar`/`pvisible`. `Object Ownership` (its own tier past Advanced, in Extended Reading, last): the engine mechanism, verified behavior table, native persistent fields, key-namespace technique, and other advanced patterns, kept together as one continuous story. Also spun off [ticket 34 on the Ew Wiki Real Build map](../ew-wiki-real-build/issues/34-colon-to-dash-title-consistency.md): 5 other Advanced pages use a colon title style that turned out to be the real outlier against the site's dash convention.
- [Build "Advanced Functions - Player Identity" page](issues/02-build-player-identity-page.md) — Built, signed off, live: `pid`/`cid` focus, `pname` as its own section with a real multi-word-name `<par_X>`-vs-`<rest_X>` gotcha, `platform`/`pvisible` briefly, `pchar` dropped entirely. Wired into the Advanced sidebar (last).
- [Build pid/ownership verification hook](issues/04-build-ownership-verification-hook.md) — Built and wired into both harnesses: `guard-pid-ownership-ledger.cjs` blocks-and-asks on any `.md`/`.mdx` edit whose new text mentions locked pid/ownership vocabulary, pointing at `EWP_pid_ownership_code_findings.md`. Documented in `docs/agents/hooks-vs-rules.md`. The companion `AGENTS.md` rule (spec §8) is still drafted-not-landed.
- [Nail down the real creation-time ownership rule](issues/05-creation-time-ownership-rule.md) — Not a separate "creation" rule — a carve-out inside the Zone Host rule. Two rules, not three: **The Zone Host** (universal fallback, re-checked every couple of seconds) with one exception (a builder gets immediate ownership, held until they leave), and **The Claiming Player** (a bonus rule some objects have — repeatable interaction claims). Locked reader-facing wording for both rules is in the ticket's Answer, ready for ticket 06 to place. The `<long_creator>`/`<long_owner>` pairing question stays open, deferred to ticket 06.
- [Rewrite explanatory sections: reader-friendly tone + reorder to the top](issues/06-explanatory-sections-rewrite.md) — Signed off. Page top is now: intro → note aside → `## There are two ways ownership can change:` heading wrapping a `<Steps>` block (Zone Host, then Claiming Player) → appendix, immediately. `<long_creator>`/`<long_owner>` cut entirely (not relocated). "Effects (FX)" carved out to its own ticket ([ticket 13](issues/13-fx-object-rule.md)). Also surfaced a real WSL dev-server staleness bug (file-watcher doesn't see Windows-side edits — needs a full restart, not a reload) worth a standing note in `ew_wiki/AGENTS.md`.
- [Object appendix, chunk 1: Container / Trap / ItemStand / ArmorStand](issues/07-object-chunk-1.md) — Signed off. Fixed 2 real field-name bugs (ItemStand/ArmorStand) and 1 factual error (Trap's ownership-transfer state), confirmed `prefab: <Component>` value-group targeting is real EWP syntax via EWP's own source (not just this wiki's docs), and locked a house-style checklist (name "ownership" explicitly, "targeting" not "watching", full-sentence sub-title links, standardized check-command text, bare component-name headings) that chunks 2-6 and the FX ticket must follow.
- [Object appendix, chunk 2: ItemDrop / Sign / Sadle / Vagon](issues/08-object-chunk-2.md) — Signed off. Found ItemDrop's `pickedUp` field is real, EWP-native syntax (EWP auto-unpacks it from the base game's packed `itemData` blob — I'd wrongly rejected it twice checking only the base game) and Vagon's `attachJoint` is a real owner-gated field usable as a direct trigger (replacing an on-demand poke-check). Added 2 more house-style rules: bad-cop `<Aside type="caution">` boxes must open with the exact words "Don't do this:", and never state an unproven claim as settled fact inside one — say "we suspect" and stop there, no unrequested hedging added on top of the maintainer's own wording.
- [Object appendix, chunk 3: Ship / Portal / Turret / CookingStation](issues/09-object-chunk-3.md) — Signed off, 3 of 4 objects (Portal dropped, see Out of scope). Corrected 3 of my own wrong mechanism guesses by checking EWP's own patch source (`HandleChanged.cs`) directly, not just its docs or the base game alone — `type: change, owner` never fires (ownership isn't a tracked field), Ship's `user` field is who's steering not who owns it, Portal's `tag`/`tagauthor` change on the wrong object entirely. Fixed Turret's wording (feeding a trophy, not "editing a list") and confirmed CookingStation's `slotstatus0/1/2` over the messier dual-typed `slot0/1/2`. Standardized the report `command:` line to one exact string across the *entire* page, not just this ticket's sections.
- [Object appendix, chunk 4: Fireplace / Fish / Gibber / TerrainModifier](issues/10-object-chunk-4.md) — Signed off, all 4 objects in. Confirmed Gibber's ownership hand-off happens a few seconds after breaking apart, right as `HandleDestroyed.cs` deletes it (`type: destroy`, not "at break-apart" as first guessed), and confirmed TerrainModifier is spawned fresh per edit — not a shared/static object — via `TerrainModifier.cs:149-151`, so it now uses a direct `type: create` trigger instead of the old on-demand `_TerrainCompiler` poke-check. Also found (but didn't add to the page) a second TerrainModifier spawn path, `Attack.SpawnOnHitTerrain`, logged in the ledger only. Removed the `allTerraformingFX` sub-example as out of scope for this entry.
- [Turn "Effects (FX)" into its own object-rule entry](issues/13-fx-object-rule.md) — Signed off. Split into two real appendix entries: `### Personal FX` (weapon hit spark = attacker-owned; everything else on your own body, caused by you or done to you, is yours; DoT FX like burning is the one exception, called out as a Caution) and a new `### Projectile` entry (player-fired = same attacker-owned mechanism as the hit spark, with a Tip on its own FX/spawns and a Caution that monster-fired instead follows Zone Host). The old broadcast-effect case moved into the Feast entry, which now explains it standalone.
- [Object appendix, chunk 5: Tameable / Character-Ragdoll / OfferingBowl](issues/11-object-chunk-5.md) — Signed off, but restructured away from its original 3 entries by maintainer redirect. Tameable folded into the Sadle entry as a one-line Tip (riding claims ownership, commanding/following never does). Character/Ragdoll removed from the page entirely. OfferingBowl removed as its own entry, its real mechanism surviving as a new Personal FX scenario ("Sacrificing at a boss altar") naming all 6 altar-using bosses split by real summon mechanic — One-Step Sacrifice (Eikthyr, The Elder, Bonemass) vs. Two-Step Offering (Moder, Yagluth, Fader) — confirmed via `OfferingBowl.cs` plus the maintainer's own real-game testing, since it's nested as a child component the CSV prefab sweep can't see on the 6 real bosses.
- [Object appendix, chunk 6: Smelter / Beehive / Piece-WearNTear / Feast](issues/12-object-chunk-6.md) — Signed off, then all 4 removed from the page by maintainer's own call — none demonstrated anything beyond the baseline Zone Host rule already taught at the top of the page (or, for Feast, already covered by earlier work this session). Same shape as chunk 5: closes with 0 standalone entries. This was the last object-appendix chunk — the appendix itself is now done.
- [Build "Object Ownership" page](issues/03-build-object-ownership-page.md) — **Split, not built.** One-pass build-then-review let mistakes stack (internal/research-facing tone leaking into reader prose, an unclear creation-ownership claim, wrong section order) go unreviewed for multiple rounds. Replaced by 8 smaller tickets below, each requiring the maintainer's own signoff before the next one starts.
