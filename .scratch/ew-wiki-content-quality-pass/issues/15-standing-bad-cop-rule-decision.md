## Question

This map's Notes floated, then deliberately deferred, a standing rule: "every concept page gets a bad-cop counterexample where one exists." The deferral's own stated condition was "revisit once the sweep shows how many spots actually exist and how the two seed examples land" — the sweep (ticket 05, 18 items) and all 8 resulting bad-cop tickets (06-13) are now closed. That condition is met; decide the standing rule for real (`/wayfinder` review, 2026-09-14, closing a fog item that's sat un-ticketed since 2026-09-12).

Given the sweep produced a real, bounded number (18) rather than an open-ended one, and all 18 are now either shipped or (pending [ticket 14](14-recheck-sweep-report-claims.md)) rechecked — does a standing "every page gets one where applicable" rule still make sense going forward, or was the sweep's one-time pass sufficient and new bad-cop examples should just get proposed opportunistically as gaps are noticed (the same way this map itself, and the player-identity/richtext/etc. findings on the Ew Wiki Real Build map, got surfaced)?

## Answer

**Decided with the maintainer, live (2026-09-16): no standing rule.** The 18-item sweep was a one-time pass with a bounded, now-closed result. Future bad-cop examples get proposed opportunistically as real gaps are noticed while writing or reviewing other content — the same way this whole map, and the player-identity/richtext findings on Ew Wiki Real Build, originally surfaced — rather than as a mandatory checklist item enforced on every future page.

**Check reach**: no `ew_wiki/AGENTS.md` change needed. The existing WRONG/CORRECT house-style convention (Notes above) stays exactly as-is for whenever a bad-cop example genuinely applies; this decision only rules out turning it into a mandatory per-page gate. Nothing to write elsewhere — a negative decision like this lives fully in this ticket and the map's own Decisions-so-far.
